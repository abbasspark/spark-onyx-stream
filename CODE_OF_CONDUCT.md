# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Everyone who takes part in this project, in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space, is expected to treat others with respect.

Expected behavior:

- Be patient and constructive, especially with newcomers.
- Criticize ideas and code, not people.
- Accept that others may disagree, and settle disagreements with technical arguments.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.

Unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Discriminatory language or imagery of any kind.
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission.
- Sexual language, imagery or unwelcome advances.
- Deliberately disrupting discussions.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove comments, commits and other contributions that break this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone who behaves in a way that is inappropriate, threatening or harmful.

## Reporting

To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through the contact details on their GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially.
